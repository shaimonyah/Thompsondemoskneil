Help & Guidelines

Quick instructions for reliable inventory records and reservations.

How to reserve materials

1STEP

Open Schedule Materials

Use the left navigation or the Create request button on the dashboard.

2STEP

Enter event details

Provide the activity name, requester, date, start and end time, and location.

3STEP

Choose available materials

Check the availability hint and enter a quantity no greater than the available stock.

4STEP

Submit and review confirmation

Keep the request details and follow up with an authorized SK official for approval.
